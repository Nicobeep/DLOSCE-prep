Limits of this study tool

  • Question bank: 500 independent DLOSCE-style question items grouped into 217 underlying clinical scenario families (296 single-answer, 195 multiple-select and 9 prescription tasks). These are original educational exercises, not official ADEX questions or validated exam predictors.
  • Clinical images: authentic, but not DLOSCE test images. Online access is required for Wikimedia image display.
  • Clinical accuracy: the general notes follow clinical guidelines listed above, but they are educational summaries. Confirm updated drug recommendations and exact examination rules.
  • Official 3D: external official Prometric training simulator, not a homebuilt substitute.
  • No restricted content: no DAT Booster content, reconstructed protected exam items, or confidential material has been included.
