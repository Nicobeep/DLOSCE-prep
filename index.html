Official DLOSCE 3D model practice

The previous synthetic tooth model has been removed. For realistic dental spatial orientation and model controls, use the official Prometric 3D tutorial linked by ADEX. The exam models are lifelike, interactive and may contain supragingival margins; they cannot be reconstructed authentically with a simplified custom mesh.

Open official 3D model practice

Five deliberate exercises

  1. Rotate from the occlusal view to proximal views and identify anatomical orientation.
  2. Locate preparation finish line and evaluate continuity, if present in model.
  3. Assess occlusal reduction and undercuts from multiple angles.
  4. Zoom into contacts and regions that may be difficult in 2D.
  5. Reset orientation and practice moving quickly and deliberately.

The official sample model may require a desktop computer and compatible browser.
